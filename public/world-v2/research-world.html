P01 · Paper · 2001

Procedural Modeling of Cities — Parish & Müller ↗

City generation separates the street network, lots and buildings.

Our decision: Use one coherent road, building parcels and waterfront boundary; do not scatter unrelated buildings.

Applied to layout; no L-system city solver implemented
P02 · Paper · 2003

Instant Architecture — Wonka et al. ↗

Split grammars organize buildings through parameterized subdivisions and matching attributes.

Our decision: Divide each facade into storeys and bays, then instantiate piers, lintels and openings with explicit dimensions.

Applied as a small deterministic module system; not the full paper algorithm
P03 · Paper · 2006

Procedural Modeling of Buildings — Müller et al. ↗

Hierarchical building rules and context constraints coordinate massing and facade details.

Our decision: Place entrances at pavement level, preserve real openings, connect balcony slabs and rails, and build parapets around roofs.

Applied to architecture
P04 · Paper · 2007

Image-based Procedural Modeling of Facades — Müller et al. ↗

Facade structure can be represented through a semantic hierarchy of floors, tiles and details.

Our decision: Use consistent window rhythm with controlled curtain and shop variations.

Hierarchy applied; no photo reconstruction or automatic facade inference claimed
P05 · Paper · 2008

Interactive Procedural Street Modeling — Chen et al. ↗

Street design can incorporate boundaries, terrain and local editing constraints.

Our decision: Constrain streets and parcels to the shoreline; keep the promenade continuous.

Layout principle applied; no tensor-field street solver implemented
P06 · Course notes · 2012

Physically-Based Shading at Disney — Burley ↗

Physically based shading connects material parameters to measured surface behavior.

Our decision: Separate dielectric plaster, glass and masonry from exposed metal; vary roughness and normals instead of adding arbitrary geometry.

Applied using standard glTF PBR materials, not a custom Disney BRDF
P07 · Course notes · 2014; revised 2015

Moving Frostbite to Physically Based Rendering — Lagarde & de Rousiers ↗

Material authoring, lighting, exposure and validation belong to one consistent rendering workflow.

Our decision: Use separate color and linear data maps, shared exposure, and directional sun with cool ambient light.

Author overview reviewed; asset/preview workflow applied. Full 54 MB course PDF was not read end to end
U01 · Unity guide · Unity 6.6 / 6000.6

URP Lit shader material Inspector ↗

Lit supports opaque and transparent surfaces plus metallic/specular workflows; unused variants add rendering cost.

Our decision: Use shared PBR surfaces and transparent recessed glazing; later map these to URP Lit.

glTF materials applied; URP material import not yet verified
U02 · Unity guide · Unity 6.6 / 6000.6

Normal maps ↗

Normal maps add lighting detail to surface shading without changing the silhouette.

Our decision: Use CC0 plaster, brick, concrete and paving normal maps at consistent metre scale.

Applied to exported material maps; Unity normal texture import pending
U03 · Unity guide · Unity 6.6 / 6000.6

Occlusion maps ↗

Occlusion maps attenuate indirect lighting and reflections, rather than replacing direct-light shadows.

Our decision: Keep direct shadows separate; do not bake black grime over every surface.

Design constraint applied; unique baked AO maps not generated
U04 · Unity guide · Unity 6.6 / 6000.6

Metallic and specular values of real surfaces ↗

Material charts distinguish metallic reflectance from dielectric behavior.

Our decision: Set masonry/plaster/glass metalness to zero; use high metalness only for exposed metal.

Applied to material definitions
U05 · Unity guide · Unity 6.6 / 6000.6

Reflection Probes ↗

Probes capture surrounding environments and can blend between different reflection regions.

Our decision: Plan separate baked probes for the street, lobby and waterfront; browser inspection uses an approximate environment.

Unity configuration pending Editor verification

U07 · Unity guide · Unity 6.6 / 6000.6

LOD Group component ↗

LOD Groups choose mesh detail levels and optionally cross-fade transitions.

Our decision: Export distinct LOD1 building meshes; retain major silhouettes while dropping interior and small roof detail.

Reduced meshes exported; Unity LODGroup wiring and thresholds pending
U08 · Unity guide · Unity 6.6 / 6000.6

Occlusion culling ↗

Occlusion culling excludes renderers completely hidden behind other geometry.

Our decision: Keep buildings and facade floors as separate chunks rather than merging the entire city into one renderer.

Chunk structure exported; Unity occlusion bake pending
U09 · Unity guide · Unity 6.6 / 6000.6

Set up scene and lights for baking ↗

Static light baking needs suitable meshes, UVs and lighting settings; moving objects need indirect-light support.

Our decision: Plan static GI for architecture and APV for characters in URP. Do not use the page’s legacy LPPV instructions for this URP project.

Unity configuration pending Editor verification

Generate lightmap UVs ↗

Lightmap UVs need non-overlapping charts and padding, separately from tiled surface UVs.

Our decision: Keep metre-scaled tiled material UVs now; generate a separate secondary UV set in the Unity import pass.

Surface UVs applied; secondary lightmap unwrap pending

Directional shadow cascades distribute shadow resolution over camera distance.

Our decision: Start with two cascades, 60 m shadow distance and 2048 atlas; profile before increasing. CPU previews use their own shadow map.

SRP Batcher reduces CPU material-state preparation for compatible shader variants.

Shared material design applied; Unity profiler verification pending
U15 · Unity guide · Unity 6.6 / 6000.6

Introduction to GPU instancing ↗

Instancing can draw repeated compatible mesh/material pairs; benefits depend on platform and render path.

Our decision: Keep repeated palm/prop sources reusable. Measure SRP batching versus instancing rather than enabling both indiscriminately.

Prototype geometry exported; instanced Unity renderers pending

GPU Resident Drawer uses BatchRendererGroup and has render-path and renderer compatibility requirements.

Our decision: Evaluate only after an ordinary URP baseline runs. No performance claim from triangle count alone.

Unity configuration pending Editor verification

Mipmap streaming manages GPU texture residency under a configurable budget.

U18 · Unity guide · Unity 6.6 / 6000.6

Introduction to decals in URP ↗

URP decals project materials onto opaque geometry; atlases can reduce material cost.

Our decision: Export markings and drain details as thin meshes now. Use a later atlas/projector pass for localized stains; transparent glass does not receive decals.

Thin marking meshes applied; Decal Renderer Feature pending
